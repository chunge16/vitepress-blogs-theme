Create a professional editorial-style Chinese infographic for a WeChat article explaining long-term ETF selection standards.

Title text:
"长期值得关注的ETF，看这5点"

Layout:
- 16:9 horizontal infographic.
- Central checklist or five-pillar framework.
- Use five numbered cards or connected nodes.
- Each card must have a short label and one concise explanation.
- Mobile-readable Chinese text.

Five criteria cards:
1. "核心指数"
   "优先选择纳斯达克100、标普500这类主流宽基"
2. "规模足够大"
   "50亿元以上优先，百亿级更适合长期候选"
3. "成交够活跃"
   "日均成交额1亿元以上，买卖更顺畅"
4. "费率合理"
   "同等条件下，低费率更适合长期持有"
5. "历史与稳定性"
   "成立时间、管理人、跟踪表现都要看"

Bottom takeaway text:
"不要只看收益率，长期持有更看重规模、流动性、成本和稳定性"

Visual style:
- Editorial data-report design, polished and trustworthy.
- Refined financial palette: off-white background, deep navy headings, muted teal, restrained gold accents.
- Use simple icons: compass/index, scale/size, exchange arrows, percent sign, timeline shield.
- No exaggerated profit imagery, no cash piles, no rockets.
- Clear hierarchy, enough whitespace.
