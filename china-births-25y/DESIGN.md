## Style Prompt

Dark editorial data journalism with a restrained cinematic mood. The frame should feel like a sober national statistics briefing: deep charcoal canvas, precise gridlines, warm white typography, amber emphasis for the 2016 high point, and cold cyan emphasis for the 2025 low point. Motion should be measured, crisp, and legible, with chart growth and callout transitions carrying the story.

## Colors

- Canvas: #090D12
- Panel: #111821
- Grid: #263241
- Primary text: #F4F1EA
- Secondary text: #9BA7B4
- Baseline bars: #6D7886
- Peak accent: #F2B35D
- Low accent: #5ED5F1
- Alert accent: #E85C4A

## Typography

- Use system Chinese sans-serif stack: "PingFang SC", "Microsoft YaHei", "Noto Sans CJK SC", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif.
- Use tabular numerals where possible for numeric labels.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use bright rainbow palettes or decorative gradients.
- Do not use casual cartoon styling.
- Do not overcrowd the chart with every value label at once.
- Do not use cards inside cards.
- Do not hide data source attribution.
